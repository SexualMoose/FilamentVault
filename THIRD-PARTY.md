## Reference data
- `app/src/main/assets/defaults.json` — 291 print-setting profile entries bundled with the app (287 reach
  the database; 4 duplicate keys collapse on import). It contains
  filament manufacturer names (e.g. Bambu Lab, Prusament, Polymaker, eSUN) used descriptively to say which
  brand a profile applies to. Those names are trademarks of their owners; FilamentVault is not affiliated with
  or endorsed by any of them. The source of the numeric values is not documented in the repo.

## Not present
No vendored or ported source code, no copyleft (GPL/LGPL/MPL/AGPL) components, and no bundled third-party
fonts, models, or artwork. The launcher icons are original vector drawables.
